# PokerVerse — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 6 October 2026
**Developer:** Hakaru Games (Hakaru Infotech), India
**Contact:** info@hakaruinfotech.com · +91 81021 16019

This Privacy Policy explains what information **PokerVerse** (the "Game", "we", "us") collects, why, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. It applies to the PokerVerse app on Android and any related services.

PokerVerse is a **free, play-money poker game**. There is **no real-money gambling**: chips have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold, cashed out or exchanged for anything of value.

---

## 1. Who can play

PokerVerse is intended **only for adults (18+)**, or the legal age for simulated gambling games where you live, if higher. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the Game, contact us and we will delete their data.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### a) Information you give us
| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| **Display name** you choose (and the avatar you pick) | Shown to other players at tables, on the friends list and in rankings. Your **friend code** is your display name plus a number (e.g. `Sam#1234`). |
| **Table chat messages and quick emotes** | Delivered to the other players at your table in real time. |
| **Friend requests, friends list and table invites ("challenges")** | To let you add friends and invite them to private tables. |

### b) Information collected automatically
| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| **Player ID** — a random identifier created when you first open the Game (no name, email or phone needed) | To keep your account, friends and multiplayer seat working. |
| **Online status / activity** (e.g. "Online · In the lobby", "At a table") | Shown to your friends only. |
| **Gameplay data** — tables joined, hands played, actions, chip balances, game statistics, recently-played players | To run the game, settle hands fairly, show your stats and let you add people you played with. |
| **Network data** — IP address and connection information | Needed to connect players in online matches. Used for the connection; we do not use it to locate you. |
| **Device and diagnostic data** — device model, OS version, app version, crash and performance reports | To find and fix crashes and bugs. |

### c) If you sign in with Google
If you choose **Continue with Google**, Google Play Games Services confirms your identity to us and gives us your **Google Play Games player ID**. We do **not** receive your Google password, and we do not receive your contacts.

Signing in links your Google Play Games account to your PokerVerse player ID so you can recover your account on another device.

### d) Stored on your device
Settings, your local chip balance and stats, tutorial progress and similar preferences are saved on your phone. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data removes them.

### e) What we do NOT collect
We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, payment or card details, or government IDs.

The Game has no in-app purchases. The "free chips" reward shows an in-game promo screen; no third-party advertising network is used.

---

## 3. How we use information
- To operate the Game: accounts, matchmaking, tables, chat, friends and rankings.
- To keep play fair and secure, and to prevent cheating, abuse and fraud.
- To fix bugs, improve stability and improve the Game.
- To respond to your support requests.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

---

## 4. Who we share information with
| Recipient | What & why |
|---|---|
| **Other players** | Your display name, friend code, avatar, table actions, chat messages, and (for friends) your online status. |
| **Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services)** — Authentication, Lobby, Relay, Friends, and Unity crash/diagnostics reporting | Our service providers that host accounts, multiplayer connections and friends, and process crash reports on our behalf. See Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy |
| **Google (Google Play Games Services)** — only if you sign in with Google | To verify your sign-in. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| **Authorities** | If required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of players or the Game. |

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union, under their own safeguards.

---

## 5. How long we keep data
- **Account data** (player ID, display name, friends, linked Google sign-in) is kept while your account exists.
- **Online-match data** such as table state and lobbies is temporary and removed after the match ends.
- **Chat messages** are relayed live to players at the table; we do not keep a chat history.
- **Crash and diagnostic reports** are kept for a limited period as set by our provider.

If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your data within **30 days**. Records we must keep by law may be kept longer.

---

## 6. Your choices and rights
- **Play as a guest:** Google sign-in is optional.
- **Change your display name** at any time in Settings.
- **Remove friends** or decline requests on the Friends screen.
- **Delete your data / account:** email **info@hakaruinfotech.com** with the subject "PokerVerse data deletion" and include your friend code (shown on the Friends screen, e.g. `Sam#1234`). We will delete your account and associated data.
- Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your data, or to object to some processing. This includes rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the GDPR (EU/UK) and US state privacy laws. Contact us to use these rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

---

## 7. Security
We use the security measures provided by our service providers, including encrypted connections to Unity Gaming Services, to protect your information. No online service is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and fix issues quickly.

## 8. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Effective date" above and, for important changes, let you know in the Game.
